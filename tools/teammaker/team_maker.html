Team Maker

Design a club for MVP Baseball 2005 (recompiled): name, colours, logos and uniforms. Export it and add it to a league pack.

Portland Pioneers
POR
Replaces: Tampa Bay Rays

The game has 30 big-league clubs. Your club takes over one of them: its schedule, division and ballpark.

Classic combinations

Primary is the cap, piping and lettering. Secondary outlines letters and numbers. Accent fills badges and trim.

Badge
Emblem

The game's own uniforms are the pattern: folds, collar and stitching stay; colours, letters and logos are yours.

Fences (feet)
Shapes

The game can't build a new ballpark, so yours is one of its own, old or new, with your name and your fences: the outfield, the wall and the stands behind it move to the distances you set. Wall heights and the numbers painted on the wall stay as they are.

Saves POR-team.zip: the logos as PNG, the uniform settings and the club's details.

Then: put the file in the teams folder of the game folder and press Build My League on the Eras page (F1 menu or launcher). Or by hand:
